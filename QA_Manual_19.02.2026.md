# 🧠 My Second Brain

Welcome to your new Second Brain! This vault is organized using the **PARA Method** (Projects, Areas, Resources, Archive), designed to keep your notes action-oriented and scalable.

## 📂 Folder Structure

- **00 Inbox**: For quick capture and unprocessed notes. Use this to reduce friction when adding new information.
- **10 Projects**: For active projects with a specific deadline or goal (e.g., "Launch Website", "Finish Course X").
- **20 Areas**: For ongoing responsibilities and maintenance (e.g., "Health", "Finances", "Work", "Home").
- **30 Resources**: For topics of interest and reference materials that don't belong to a specific project or area (e.g., "Recipes", "Code Snippets", "Travel Ideas").
- **40 Archive**: For completed projects, inactive areas, or resources no longer relevant.
- **90 Meta**: For internal system management:
   - **Templates**: For Obsidian templates (e.g., Daily Note, Project Template).
   - **Attachments**: For images, PDFs, and other non-markdown files.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start
1. **Capture**: Throw everything into `00 Inbox` to keep your mind clear.
2. **Process**: Once a week (or when you have time), move notes from `00 Inbox` to the appropriate folder.
3. **Action**: Focus your work in `10 Projects`.
4. **Learn**: Store knowledge in `30 Resources`.
